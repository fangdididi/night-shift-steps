# Replace the backup disk on the office NAS

You need the new disk, a Phillips screwdriver and 30 minutes. The shared drive is not available during this job.

1. Tell the office that the shared drive stops for 30 minutes.
2. Log in to the NAS admin page.
3. Go to the **Storage** page.
4. Select **Disk 2**.
Result: The status of Disk 2 shows "Failing". If it shows "Healthy", stop and read Recovery A.
WARNING: Power off the NAS before you remove a disk. A disk removed while it runs can corrupt the second disk.
5. Push the power button for 2 seconds to power off the NAS.
Result: All the lights on the front go off after about 1 minute.
CAUTION: The old disk can be hot after use. Hold it by the tray, or you can burn your fingers.
6. Pull out the tray for Disk 2.
7. Put the new disk in the tray.
8. Push the tray in until it clicks.
9. Push the power button once to start the NAS.
Result: On the **Storage** page, Disk 2 shows "Rebuilding".

## Recovery A

If Disk 2 shows "Healthy", do not remove it. Send a screenshot of the Storage page to the IT contact.
